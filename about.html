About

I'm Jacob Perry, a motion designer and 3D animator based in Toronto. I make motion graphics in After Effects, build and animate scenes in Blender, and have a background in programming that I'm starting to bring into my motion work. I studied fine art at NSCAD University and programming at George Brown College, and before this I painted scenic finishes for film and television with IATSE 873 and ran bar teams as a general manager. Those jobs taught me to work to a deadline and to someone else's brief. I'm happiest when a piece is a problem to solve: getting the timing right, making one shape hand off to the next, or working out how to make a 2D drawing live in a 3D room.
