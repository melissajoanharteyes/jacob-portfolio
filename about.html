About

I'm Jacob Perry — an artist and creative technologist based in Toronto. Over the past decade I've led bar teams as a general manager, painted scenic finishes for film and television with IATSE 873, and built a technical practice spanning illustration, 3D, and software development. I hold a BFA from NSCAD University and a diploma in Computer Analysis and Programming from George Brown College, and I gravitate toward work that sits at the intersection of craft, systems, and people — building structured process out of ambiguous problems, whether that's a bar's inventory system or a piece of pixel art.
